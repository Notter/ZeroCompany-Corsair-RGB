Corsair RGB Keyboard Integration - Star Wars Zero Company

This is an unofficial community mod, not an official Corsair, Star Wars,
Lucasfilm, Electronic Arts or Bit Reactor product.

Bundled third-party runtime: Corsair iCUE SDK 4.0.84,
iCUESDK.x64_2019.dll, distributed unchanged from Corsair's redist directory.
Official source: https://github.com/CorsairOfficial/cue-sdk/releases/tag/v4.0.84
The accompanying Corsair-iCUE-SDK-Reference-and-EULA.pdf is the original SDK
documentation and contains Corsair's End User License Agreement.
Corsair's runtime remains subject to its own terms.

UE4SS and the game-specific UE4SS loader are separate dependencies and are
not included. Game files, test runtimes, captures and saved-game data are
not included.
